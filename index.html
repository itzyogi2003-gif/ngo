Transparency & Governance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our legal registration, donations, monument protocols, and field operations.

Why is a Section 8 Company recommended over a traditional Trust? ▼
A Section 8 Company incorporated under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) offers the highest standard of corporate governance, digital auditing, and nationwide operational scope across India. Unlike regional trusts which often face jurisdiction limitations, a Section 8 entity easily registers for CSR-1 funding from large corporations and maintains strict public financial disclosures.
How do volunteers handle protected historical monuments legally? ▼
Under India's Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains (AMASR) Act, monument conservation is strictly regulated by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI). Volunteers of Hind Raksha Samiti NEVER use abrasive chemicals, mechanical scrubbers, or alter stones independently. Our activities focus strictly on non-invasive ground litter cleaning, anti-vandalism vigilance reporting, and public education under local administrative permissions.
Are donations eligible for Section 80G tax deductions? ▼
Yes. Under the Income Tax Act, Hind Raksha Samiti applies for provisional registration under Section 12AB (exemption for NGO income) and Section 80G (50% taxable income deduction for Indian donors). An official certificate containing the NGO's Unique Registration Number (URN) is issued for your tax returns.
How can college students and working professionals volunteer? ▼
We offer flexible engagement models! You can join weekend tree plantation drives, participate in street animal feeding & rabies vaccination rounds in your local neighborhood, or contribute remotely through legal RTI drafting, photography, and social media awareness campaigns. All active volunteers receive an official Certificate of Seva.
