# Hind Raksha Samiti (हिंद रक्षा समिति) 🇮🇳

**सेवा • संरक्षण • विरासत**  
*Protect Animals • Preserve Nature • Safeguard Heritage*

Hind Raksha Samiti is a national non-profit portal uniting multiple charitable impact sectors across India:
- **Animal Protection & Rescue Patrol (जीव रक्षा)**: 24/7 rescue ambulances, anti-rabies vaccination, ABC sterilization, and cruelty prevention.
- **Environmental & Forest Conservation (पर्यावरण संरक्षण)**: Afforestation of native Peepal, Banyan, and Neem trees, Miyawaki urban micro-forests, and anti-plastic drives.
- **Historical Heritage Protection (धरोहर संरक्षण)**: Anti-vandalism vigilance, historic stepwell (baoli) rejuvenation, and ASI-compliant monument cleanups.
- **Water Conservation (जल संरक्षण)**: Traditional pond and stepwell desiltation, rainwater harvesting, and riverbank ecological buffer zones.
- **Youth Leadership & Civic Seva (युवा शक्ति)**: Student Yuva Mandals, disaster first-aid workshops, and civic education.
- **Cultural Revival & Folk Traditions (सांस्कृतिक पुनर्जागरण)**: Archiving ancient knowledge, supporting traditional artisans, and Vedic environmental values.

---

## 🌟 Features
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless instant English ⇄ हिन्दी toggle.
- **Theme Modes**: Modern Dark and Light themes with automatic preference persistence.
- **24/7 SOS Incident Dispatch**: Community reporting for animal distress, monument vandalism, and tree felling with ticket generation.
- **Donation & 80G Tax Exemption Hub**: Tiered donation calculator with simulated UPI QR code and provisional Section 80G tax receipt generator.
- **90-Day Execution Roadmap**: Step-by-step institutional plan (Foundation, Registration, Field Drives, National Scaling).
- **Volunteer Enrollment**: Interactive volunteer registration with instant Rashtriya Sevak Digital ID Card.
